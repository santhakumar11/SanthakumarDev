A good interface doesn’t need to shout. Sometimes the things that make a website feel welcoming are the quiet ones: clear language, a little breathing room, and a page that works just as well on a phone.

## Thoughtfulness is in the details

Design is more than colors and type. It’s helping someone find their way, making the next step obvious, and being careful not to get in their way.

I’m trying to bring that same care to the things I build. It’s a practice, not a finish line, and every small improvement counts.
