The internet moves quickly. I wanted a small place that could move at its own pace.

This blog is a notebook for the things I’m learning: ideas from a project, a useful detail I might otherwise forget, or a question I’m still figuring out. Writing helps me slow down enough to understand what I think.

## Small notes, shared openly

Each post here is written in Markdown and lives alongside the site itself. That keeps publishing simple: write, save, and let GitHub Pages take care of the rest.

I hope some of these notes are useful to you, too. And if nothing else, perhaps they’ll remind both of us that there’s always something new worth exploring.
