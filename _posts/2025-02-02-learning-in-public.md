It’s tempting to wait until you know enough before you share what you’re learning. But the unfinished questions are often the most interesting part.

## Progress beats polish

Explaining an idea makes you notice where your understanding is thin. A short note, a tiny demo, or an honest question can invite feedback that changes your direction for the better.

Learning in public isn’t about having all the answers. It’s about being open about the process: what you tried, what surprised you, and what you’d do differently next time.

So here’s to sharing the rough draft, asking the question, and getting a little better as we go.
